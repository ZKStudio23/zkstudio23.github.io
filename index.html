In short:

1. Data stored only on your device

The following data is saved locally on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Settings: selected country and currency, weight unit, theme and similar preferences
  • Gold and silver holdings you enter, and your Zakat calculator inputs
  • Savings goals and their reminder settings
  • Price alerts you create
  • Cached prices, exchange rates, charts and news, so the app works offline
  • Ad-related state, such as when your last ad-free reward started

Because this data never leaves your device, Google Play does not count it as "collected".

2. Data collected and shared

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads. To show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device or other identifiers: your Android advertising ID, and on supported devices, Android Privacy Sandbox signals (Topics and attribution)
  • Approximate location: worked out from your IP address
  • App activity: ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics: ad-related performance and crash information
  • Device information: model, operating system, language and app version

This data is collected by Google and handled under its policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Our data server (Cloudflare Worker)

The app gets gold and silver prices, exchange rates, price charts and news through a server we run on Cloudflare. Like any web server, it receives your IP address when the app connects. It uses Cloudflare's network data to work out your approximate country, only so the right currency is selected for you. The server does not store your IP address, does not log identifiers, and does not build a profile of you. Cloudflare processes these requests under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Public data providers (fallback only)

If our server is unreachable, the app contacts these public data sources directly for prices, exchange rates, charts and news: gold-api.com, ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com), Yahoo Finance, FXStreet, Mining.com and Investing.com. These services then receive your IP address and standard request data, under their own privacy policies. The app sends them no personal information.

What we do not collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, videos, files, messages, precise location, health data, financial account details or payment information. The app has no in-app purchases and does not process payments.

4. Advertising

  • The app is free and supported by ads. Ads are clearly separate from app content.
  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of screens. Interstitial ads may appear between screens, with limits on how often. An app-open ad may appear when you open or return to the app.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch one in return for a short ad-free period. Every feature of the app works without watching them.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app shows Google's consent message before it requests any ads. There you can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or decline where the option exists.
  • The advertising ID is used only for advertising and analytics. It is not linked to any personal information or to other device identifiers.

6. How we use data

  • To provide the app's features: live rates, calculators, charts, news, alerts and reminders
  • To preselect your local currency
  • To show and measure ads that keep the app free
  • To prevent ad fraud and abuse (done by Google)

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Consent: personalised advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device for ads, through Google's consent message.
  • Legitimate interests: providing the app's features, choosing your currency, non-personalised ads, and fraud prevention.

8. International transfers

Google, Cloudflare and the data providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They use their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for these transfers.

9. Security

All network connections use HTTPS encryption. We don't run a user database and don't keep personal data on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit risk by collecting as little as possible.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings → Apps → Gold Rate & Zakat Calculator → Storage → Clear data), or uninstall the app.
  • Our server: keeps nothing about you. Requests are processed and discarded.
  • Google AdMob: keeps data according to Google's retention policies. You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in Android settings.
  • Account deletion: the app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: where it applies, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app to change your consent at any time.
  • Personalised ads: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Notifications: remove your alerts and reminders in the app, or turn off notifications in Android's app settings.
  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We hold no personal data ourselves, so for data held by Google, use Google's privacy tools.
  • US states (e.g. California): we do not sell personal information. Where the law treats ad personalisation as "sharing", you can opt out through the consent message or Ad privacy choices in the app, or by deleting your advertising ID.

12. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

13. Links to other websites

14. Financial and religious disclaimer

Prices are for information only. They may be delayed and may differ from rates offered by local jewellers, banks or dealers. The app does not offer trading, investment, loans or any financial service, and nothing in it is financial advice. The Zakat calculator gives an estimate based on the values you enter. For religious rulings on your situation, consult a qualified scholar.
